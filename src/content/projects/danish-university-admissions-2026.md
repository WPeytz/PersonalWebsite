## Overview

Danish University Admissions 2026 turns the national coordinated admissions
results into an interactive, bilingual atlas. It brings together headline
figures, institutional comparisons, programme demand, admission cutoffs, and
open study places in one explorable view.

The project makes a large public dataset easier to navigate by showing where
student ambition meets the available capacity across Danish higher education.

## What it explores

- National admissions and first-choice demand
- Comparisons across institutions and subject areas
- Programme-level admission cutoffs
- Geographic patterns across Denmark
- Open study places and remaining capacity
