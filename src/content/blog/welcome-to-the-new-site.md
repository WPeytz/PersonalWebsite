Welcome to the new version of **williampeytz.com**. I rebuilt the site from
scratch as a single place to keep my projects, writing, and CV — partly
because I wanted somewhere clean to point people toward, and partly
because I needed a venue for writing as I work through the rest of my
MSc.

## The short tour

- **[Projects](/projects)** — case studies of what I'm building now
  (Stemma is the current one) and a selection of earlier work from
  coursework and side projects.
- **[CV](/resume)** — full resume, both web and PDF.
- **[About](/about)** — who I am and what I'm currently focused on.
- **Writing** — this section. I'll use it for notes and longer-form
  pieces as they come up.

## What I expect to write about

Mostly the things I'd want to read myself: voice agents and the
engineering around them, what it actually looks like to build a startup
while doing a Master's, lessons from shipping ML to people who don't
care about the model, and the parts of my coursework that turn out to
be worth more than a hand-in.

## Stack

For the curious — the site is [Astro](https://astro.build) with Tailwind
CSS, content collections for posts and projects, and a dark-first
aesthetic borrowed in spirit from Vercel and Linear. It deploys
statically to Vercel on every push.

If you've made it this far, thanks for stopping by. The fastest way to
reach me is [email](mailto:williamhkp@gmail.com).
